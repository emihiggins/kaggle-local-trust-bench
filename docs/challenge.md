# Challenge requirements — authoritative rule summary

Checked 2026-10-04 against [landing page](https://dev.to/challenges/kaggle-2026-09-23), [contest-specific rules](https://dev.to/page/kaggle-2026-09-23-contest-rules), and [general official rules](https://dev.to/page/official-hackathon-rules). General rules explicitly prevail if there is a conflict. Recheck before submission.

## Confirmed dates and format

- Entry period: September 23, 2026, 09:00 PDT through **October 11, 2026, 23:59 PDT**.
- Closing time in UTC: **October 12, 2026, 06:59 UTC** (PDT is UTC−7). Sponsor's clock is authoritative. Aim to publish a day early.
- Build a benchmark **on Kaggle** and include its **publicly accessible benchmark link** in a published DEV submission.
- Use the provided submission template and `kagglechallenge` tag. Template additionally includes `devchallenge`, `ai`, `machinelearning`.
- Explain tasks and why they matter, models tested and why chosen, findings/insights, and where the benchmark can be inspected.
- One entry per participant; if multiple are entered, the first is the one considered. Teams up to four; one team post, list collaborators' DEV handles.
- Five winners, each $500 USD, DEV++ membership and winner badge; valid qualified entries receive a completion badge.

## What judges reward

**Insights Shared; Writing Quality; Creativity in Approach.** No numerical weighting is published. In a tie, positive reactions on the DEV post determine the winner. A large model count or maximum tokens/second is not itself a judging criterion.

Implication: emphasize a specific practical question, controlled perturbations, understandable failure cases, honest uncertainty, and what a developer should do differently after reading the results.

## Eligibility and integrity gates (entrant must confirm)

- 18+ AND legal age of majority where applicable; eligible residency, active DEV account in good standing, active email and Internet access.
- General rules exclude residents of Afghanistan, Belarus, Central African Republic, Cuba, Equatorial Guinea, Iran, Iraq, Kosovo, Libya, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Tanzania, Venezuela and Yemen, and other export-control/restricted-party cases. Also check employer/sponsor relationship restrictions directly in the official rules. No eligibility determination has been made here.
- Entry development must have started within the entry period. This new project starts October 4. Reused libraries are fine; do not present pre-existing benchmark work as an original new entry.
- Original work; significant adaptations where reusing prior work; credit nontrivial sources, datasets and code. AI assistance is allowed. Write in English for prize eligibility (non-English is badge-eligible only per FAQ).
- Entrant retains ownership but grants the sponsor the license described in the general rules; The entrant must review the full terms before entering.
- No purchase necessary. Do not infer an API spend requirement.

## Submission completion checklist

- [ ] Entrant confirms eligibility and accepts the current official rules.
- [ ] One original benchmark exists as a Kaggle Benchmark entity, not merely a notebook/dataset/repo.
- [ ] Tasks have actual model runs and inspectable results; no placeholders or invented measurements.
- [ ] Benchmark URL is publicly accessible in a signed-out browser; task assets/results needed to understand it are accessible too.
- [ ] DEV post uses the official template structure and `kagglechallenge` tag.
- [ ] Article names task design, exact model identities, meaningful findings, limitations and next steps.
- [ ] Article includes the working public benchmark URL, credits and team handles if relevant.
- [ ] Publication is within the entry period; preserve DEV URL and publication time.

A Mac-only throughput report is not an adequate substitute for the required Kaggle benchmark. Private GitHub is acceptable as a planning location, but cannot be the only place judges can inspect the work.
